Portal check-ins. Everything here is off until you switch it on.

Read this before switching it on

The platforms count how often you open your orders because they are trying to measure you. If this does the looking for you, the number stops being about you — and a platform that looked closely could take that view. It is your shop and your call; this is here so you make it knowingly.

It also only works while your browser is open and you are still signed in to the portal. It cannot sign you in.

Portal check-ins

Every so often, open each portal behind whatever you are doing, click through the order tabs the way you would, and close it again.

A different, random gap each time, somewhere between those two — never the same beat over and over.

Your working hours. Nothing runs outside them.
